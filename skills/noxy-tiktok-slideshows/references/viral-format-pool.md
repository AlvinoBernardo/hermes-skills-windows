## User feedback r2 (2026-10-08), apply to all future viral packages
- ALL viral formats must be about WEIGHT LOSS. The user adopts a viral post's STYLE, not its fitness/lifting topic. The 8-things format is now `viral-8-things-weight-loss` (hook `8 Things I Wish Someone Told Me Before I Started Losing Weight`, lessons: calories, protein, keep lifting, steps, liquid calories, weekends, scale, sleep). The editorial format is `viral-editorial-lose-weight` (`how to actually lose weight as a girl`: liquid calories, carbs, protein, 10k steps flowchart, tracking, closing). The body-type format is `viral-body-type-weight-loss` (identify, wrist, endomorph keys, what to eat, day of eating with calories, daily habits, what to avoid, 3x full body, Noxy).
- Use REAL Pinterest photos wherever a real photo works. Generate only what can't be photographed (illustrations, product cutouts, green-screen phones). The user spotted a photo that looked AI-made in a real-photo slot (v1 last slide). Reject Pinterest picks that look AI-made: smooth plastic skin, generator sizes like 1086x1448.
- No blur anywhere. Never zoom out a photo onto a blurred copy of itself (the old `shrink` trick). Generate the phone shot with the phone already in the lower half instead. For illustrated cutouts, remove the background with scipy `ndimage.label` on border-connected cream pixels (PIL floodfill silently failed and left a visible rectangle), then drop any slivers smaller than 8% of the largest component.
- Small TikTok text over bright photos was hard to read. In render_photo.py use weight 600, stroke 2, shadow 0.95, sizes about 12% larger, and `scrim` 0.3 to 0.38 (a soft dark glow behind each text block).

- Editorial format (viral-editorial-lose-weight): the user found the small paragraph text hard to read. Body copy: 2 to 3 short, plain sentences (about 20 to 25 words, 3 lines at 50px), stating what, an everyday example and why. The user rejected one-liners as too short, and the original paragraph as too long and small. Avoid one-word orphan lines. Sizes: title Inter 98px, body 50px, column labels 58px (short single words when two labels share a row, e.g. `guessing` / `tracking`), flowchart boxes 52/32px with only 3 rows. Never go back to the original's tiny 23px paragraph.

## Making a NEW package of a viral format
- Copy the template folder, keep the layout and slide grammar, change the photos (new Pinterest picks via pin_tools, query always includes `aesthetic`, dHash vs library) and vary the tips while keeping the original structure and casual voice. Re-generate AI pieces (cutouts, illustrations, green-screen phones) with new outfits/scenes.
- Phones: generate with a pure #00FF00 screen, composite the REAL `assets/noxy-home-real.png`. Never ship an AI app UI.
- Keep fixed safety edits: no `burns fat`, no `100% faster`, no mean body-shaming line, competitor apps replaced by Noxy. Body-type wrist test is framed as a rough guide in the caption.
- Captions: AI disclosure when AI images are used, no on-image AI badge. Pinterest rights unverified.
- QC: check text against faces/heads in a preview (cover text placement often needs moving), and darken busy bright photos (e.g. food bowl bright 0.5) so the white text reads.
