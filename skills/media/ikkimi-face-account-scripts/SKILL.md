# Ikkimi face-account scripts (AJ the Senpai format)

Alvino's personal face account markets Ikkimi (learn Japanese from the anime you watch, ikkimi.app). **The ONLY format is AJ the Senpai's** (youtube.com/@ajthesenpai). He rejected Kito Senpai's format ("kito senpai is shit"): never suggest Kito skits, couch reactions or yaps. Project folder: `~/Desktop/Ikkimi/Ikkimi-2026-11-13-senpai-face-account/` (PLAN.md, SCRIPT-NN-*.md, research/ajthesenpai/REPORT.md; Kito material archived in `_old/kito/`).

## When to Use
"Write the next script", "make a script for [anime]", "remember this format", any face-account / personal-brand anime video for Ikkimi. For anime edits or the brand-account CTA strategy, use `ikkimi-anime-edits` instead.

## The format (user-approved)
- **Layout:** vertical split screen. Anime clips on top (~45%), his face on the bottom (~55%), plain wall, the same shirt every video. Captions 2-3 words at a time.
- **Length:** 50-75 s, fast talking, no intro.
- **Shows:** sad / cult / deep anime where the premise sells itself (AJ's hits: Paranoia Agent 1.4M, A Silent Voice 1.3M, 86 919K, Grave of the Fireflies 834K, Tatami Galaxy 828K, Mononoke 752K). Avoid trivia, lists, quizzes, "facts about [big show]" (6-30K).
- **Title:** lowercase "tell me the anime that/where ..." + 2-3 hashtags.

## Beat structure (copy this order; from AJ's last 14 shorts, `research/ajthesenpai/SCRIPTING.md`)
1. **HOOK:** premise + contradiction in 2 sentences: "There's an anime (movie) where [something that sounds wrong]... and [the twist]."
2. **MISREAD -> REAL READ:** "Everyone calls it / It looks like X. But I think it's really about Y."
3. **STORY told through questions** in his voice: "So what happens to him? ...", "So how do you say sorry to someone who can't hear you? He learns sign language." Ask, then answer; that's AJ's main story engine.
4. **TWIST**, then **THE LINE:** the real Japanese line in romaji (laptop shot), then **"That's what got me."**
5. **THE JAPANESE:** 2-3 plain lines: "It basically means '...'. Like, he's not saying X. He's saying Y."
6. **PERSONAL LINE (optional):** one true reaction in his words (AJ: "I watched it thinking about my own mom"). Never invent it; leave a bracketed placeholder.
7. **IT'S ASKING:** "It's asking [question]. And I think [answer]."
8. **PEOPLE SKIP IT:** "People skip it because they think it's just [X]."
9. **FACTS:** title (first time the name is said), length/episodes, studio, maybe where to watch or an action line ("Watch it with the Japanese audio").
10. **QUESTION:** "So tell me... what's the anime that [the title, word for word]?"
11. **CTA (verbatim, his wording):** "Oh yeah, by the way, if you want to learn Japanese while watching anime, you should go check out the Ikkimi app." On-screen text "ikkimi.app" / "link in bio" + 2 s of the app on his laptop. Never write a different CTA.
12. **SIGN-OFF:** [his sign-off] (not chosen yet; ask, or leave the placeholder).

- **He cut the Japanese line + meaning beats** (steps 4-5) from the Silent Voice script: "I don't think this is needed". Default: no Japanese-line breakdown in the story; the CTA alone carries the app. Offer it as an optional beat only.

AJ habits: don't say the show's name until FACTS (viewers name it in the comments); short "So..." sentences; rule-of-three lines ("No hugs, no handshakes, nobody close to her"); 65-75 s, ~300 spoken words. Only his STORY videos (median ~90K in his last 14), never his facts/list videos (median ~12K).

Example that passed: `SCRIPT-01-march-comes-in-like-a-lion.md`. He dropped A Silent Voice ("DONT DO SILENT VOICE"); don't propose it again. Prefer shows AJ hasn't covered (grep `research/ajthesenpai_shorts.tsv`; he already did Silent Voice, Violet Evergarden, Erased, Vinland Saga, Your Lie in April, Anohana, 86, Madoka, Grave of the Fireflies).

## Voice (he asked to remember this)
- He talks casually, like telling a friend: "So it's basically about a boy who bullied this girl... like, so bad that she ended up leaving the school... so a few years later he meets the girl again..."
- Use "so", "basically", "like", plain words, one idea per line, short and easy to understand.
- No writerly beats, symbolism tours or stacked clever lines (he rejected "The movie literally puts an X over every face..."). Keep the Japanese explanation to 2-3 lines.
- Get the show's theme from HIS reading. He rejected framing A Silent Voice as a romance misunderstanding (suki/tsuki); to him it's about a boy finding a reason to live. When a show has several readings, pick the deeper life/meaning one or ask.

## Delivery layout
Teleprompter style: labelled beats in CAPS, *(clip/shot note)* per beat, one short line each, **bold** punch words. When he says "full script", reprint the whole thing with every accepted change. Save as `SCRIPT-NN-<show>-<slug>.md` in the project folder and update PLAN.md's script list.

## Rules
- **Verify every Japanese line** with a web search (Japanese sources, e.g. 「<line>」 + show name) before writing it in; tell him to watch the scene once and say it the way the character says it.
- Never call Ikkimi "free" (paid: $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr).
- Heavy topics: say "give up on life" / "done with life", never "suicide" / "kill himself" (platforms push those videos less).
- Don't reuse AJ's own hook for the same show (check his title list in `research/ajthesenpai_shorts.tsv`).
- No trending song when the app is named; promo-content toggle on. TikTok bio link needs ~1,000 followers, so post on YouTube Shorts / Reels too.
- Never invent facts about episodes, studios or lines; mark unknowns `[check]`.
