# Anime edit engine (Ikkimi)

Engine: `~/IkkimiAssets/engine/edit_engine.py`, venv `~/IkkimiAssets/engine/.venv` (py3.12). It turns a JSON spec into a beat-synced 1080x1920 edit. Full schema + every effect param: `~/IkkimiAssets/engine/README.md`. Example specs: `engine/tests/{hype,soft,explicit60}_spec.json`.

## Motion lab (speed ramps / twixtor look, Windows PC)
`~/IkkimiAssets/engine/motion_lab/motion.py` (demo: `demo.py`, out/COMPARE_before_after.mp4). Shot dicts with eased velocity keys `speed` [(local_t, x)], `zoom`/`pan`/`rot` keys, `hits` (punch/shake/flash/chroma, exponential decay), `bounds` (the source shot's own start/end), rendered at 60 fps into the 16:9-in-9:16 canvas.
- Slow-mo = rife-ncnn-vulkan (`~/IkkimiAssets/tools/rife-ncnn-vulkan-20221029-windows`, model rife-v4.6, Vulkan on the RTX 4070 Ti SUPER) at K=8 subframes per drawing. Motion blur = average of subframes over a 180-degree shutter, so faster speed gives more blur automatically; camera moves get sub-sampled blur too. 7.3 s renders in ~2 min.
- Drop held duplicate frames before RIFE, and keep only real new drawings: characters are on 2s/3s while effects move on 1s, so a plain duplicate check leaves stepped slow-mo. Keep frames whose diff is >= 0.45x the local max.
- Never let RIFE morph across a source cut: pass the shot's `bounds` and cap `src_out`. The velocity graph is scaled down if it would run past the shot end. Cut-detect the source first (diff > ~40 at 320x180 is a cut; twixtor-ish packs have no single big diff).
- Close-up expression changes (a mouth opening) melt under RIFE. Flag a pair when the Farneback-warp residual p99.5 is > 130 while the median flow is < 8 px (still frame, big local change). Hold A, dissolve to B over the middle ~16%, then hold B. Fast zooms and dashes (median flow > 8) interpolate fine.

## Engine v2 (kinetic typography / @carlsunraku look)
`~/IkkimiAssets/engine/edit_engine_v2.py` (same CLI; v1 specs still render) + `engine/README_v2.md` (full schema + example). Adds `output.canvas:"square"` (1080 square on 9:16, all FX in square coords), `words[]` (per-letter squeeze/jitter/outline/hard shadow/hollow/karaoke `color_keys`/`typing`/slam/pop/arc/`cycle` font flicker/blend), `clips[].sticker` (torn/solid/dashed/glow outline, paper/kraft/footage bg, bob), mosaic transitions (`preset` ref1/ref2/ref3/ref5), `particles[]` sakura/leaves, light leaks, `ff_soft_vivid` / `night_pink` grades + `look.glow.tint`, per-clip `look`, `insets[]` oval flicker, `zoom_blur_pulse`, flash `hold`, `{"solid":[0,0,0]}` black slots, `audio.segments` splicing. Test specs are script-built: `engine/tests_v2/build_full_test.py`; REF-vs-V2 grids: `tests_v2/cmp_grid.py`.
- Giant didone that matched ref1 LOVE: `bodoni` wght 900, scale_x 0.40, tracking -0.05, jitter_y 0.05-0.07, stroke 4, shadow dx7 dy5, `max_width` 0.96.
- rembg: use the CoreML provider (default in v2): ~0.2-2.7 s/matte vs 21 s/frame CPU under load 70. Use `matte.reuse` 2-3 (moving) or "shot" (still) + `cache_dir`.
- Sticker subjects must be cut from the pre-bloom picture (v2 does this); a white-bg bloom hazes the cut-out otherwise.
- Oval/inset stills: pick medium CUs; cover-cropped ECUs show only skin.
- Mosaic `beat_fx` uses `from` (frame offset), not `start` (that's the trigger window).

## Landscape-in-9:16 + ref-matched typography (edit 07 draft2)
- `output.canvas: "landscape"` (+ `inner` [1920,1080], `inner_y`) = 16:9 picture centred in 1080x1920. FX/text live in the 1920 layer, so nothing can be drawn in the black bars. For bar subtitles, post-process: one PIL RGBA PNG per subtitle state, chained ffmpeg `overlay=0:Y:enable='between(t,a,b)'` with `-c:a copy` (~20 s for 30 s). Script: `edits/10-bc-refmix/work/v2/add_bar_subs.py`. For per-word typing, use the per-frame `edits/11-csm-reze-sad/work/subs_overlay.py`. `overlays[] {type:"image"}` places an RGBA PNG (title lockups drawn with PIL) with scale/fade. See README_v2.
- To copy a 16:9 ref's typography: measure each word's ink box on gridded ref frames (2x2 sheets of 480x270, no downscale; sample mid-window and step off mosaic frames), then FIT each word with the engine's own `KText` (draw on black + white 1920x1080 layers, take the ink bbox): height *= ref_h/ink_h; scale_x so the width matches (clamp didone 0.22-1.0); then shift x/y by the ink-centre error. Giant didones come out tall and narrow like the ref. Ref: edits/07-ff-her-v2/work/build_spec.py + d2/ref_words.json.
- The lbp anime-face cascade misses most episode faces (5 of 79 shots). Hand-mark face boxes from gridded clean stills (d2/faces_manual.json) for the text-on-face check. Skip frame-filling ECU boxes (>55% area), because refs lay giant words over those too.
- Behind-subject share: count the shots whose giant word is behind; promote giant words whose matte coverage of the word box is 0.1-0.6 until about 30% of shots have one (ref1 ratio).
- Ref mosaic per cut: a HF-loss detector (1 - hf/median of the neighbours) catches blurry TikTok mosaics. A block-grid comb detector only fires on the big ones. ref1 has mosaic on about 71 of 76 cuts, peaking at frames -1/0 (about 5 frames). Scripts: edits/07-ff-her-v2/work/d2/mosaic_detect*.py.
- Grey sticker/white backgrounds: global vignette `radius 0.9, softness 0.8` starts at r=0.1, so it darkens the whole frame by the strength (247->213 at 0.12). Use radius >=1.25 and softness <=0.5, and strength 0 on high-key/sticker slots.
- A clip `preset: night_pink` merged over a global grade that sets gamma/contrast/lift/black is neutered (the global tone keys win). Repeat the preset's tone keys explicitly in the clip grade.
- With a white `bg` the sticker loses footage props (NCOP ribbons). Use `bg: "footage"` when the shot is already on white.

## Footage-blended dialogue type (edit 09 type lab)
- words[] keys `alpha_ramp` (opacity ramp along the text, pre-rotate), `texture` (fill x footage luma so line art/fireworks show through), `tint_footage` (fill pulled toward the footage colour), `anchor_x: left|right`; fonts `poppins_b/sb/m/black`. Presets + rules: edits/09-dud-dub-edit/work/type_lab/STYLE_KIT.md + style_kit.json; mock any word on a still with `type_lab/lab.py` (seconds, no render).
- Place behind-subject words by measuring the real matte (rembg on the cropped frame, `EV.grab_still`) against the word's KText ink mask. Search x/y/scale until the subject covers ≤15% of the ink (≤25% for 5+ letters), else draw it on top where coverage is <6%. At 0.3-0.4 coverage only the outer letters show. Ref: edits/09-dud-dub-edit/work/build_spec_v2.py `place_search` (+ `--mock` per-word stills sheet in ~10 s). Giant white words on bright backgrounds (ink luma >185) vanish, so switch them to dark ink.
- The engine's `_NO_MUSIC` twin is silent. For dub/dialogue edits, mux the dialogue bus (`dub_only.wav`) into it with `-c:v copy`.
- Slowed reused shots (speed <1) reach further into the source: recheck `in + dur*speed` against the shot's end, because 1 frame of the next shot flashed in at 0.64x.
- isnet-anime mattes fail on dark night shots (near-empty matte) and treat window reflections as subject; check the matte before marking a word behind_subject.

## Motion lab (AE-style speed ramps, Windows PC)
- The PC has an RTX 4070 Ti SUPER 16 GB and DaVinci Resolve. It has no After Effects. GPU frame interpolation uses `~/IkkimiAssets/tools/rife-ncnn-vulkan-20221029-windows/rife-ncnn-vulkan.exe` (model rife-v4.6, `-n` = drawings x K).
- `~/IkkimiAssets/engine/motion_lab/motion.py`: eased velocity keys integrated to source time, RIFE K=8 subframes, a 180-degree motion blur (subframes averaged over source + camera motion), beat `hits` (zoom punch + decaying shake/rotation + flash + chroma) and eased zoom keys. Demo + before/after: `motion_lab/demo.py`, `out/COMPARE_before_after.mp4` (7.3 s at 60 fps renders in ~2.5 min).
- Dedupe anime holds before RIFE: keep only frames whose diff is ≥0.45x the local max (new drawings). Otherwise the 2s/3s cadence plus moving FX gives stepped slow-mo.
- Clamp each shot's prep range to the source shot bounds (`bounds`, `src_out`), because RIFE morphs across a source cut and a foreign shot bleeds in. Scale the velocity graph down when it overruns the shot.
- Known artefact: slow-mo face close-ups with big drawing changes (the mouth/teeth) smear for a few frames. Use lower slow-mo there, or hold the drawing instead.

## Workflow
1. Plan the song section: `.venv/bin/python edit_engine.py --analyze song.mp3 --start S --end E` -> tempo, beats, downbeats (times relative to S), chosen `onset_mode`, segment LUFS. Find the drop with a 0.1 s RMS scan; start the edit 1-2 beats before it.
2. Find subject positions in 16:9 sources (frame with ffmpeg `drawgrid` + vision) -> set `focus_x`/`focus_y` per clip; `focus_y` matters only when `zoom` > 1.
3. QA cheaply first: `--stills-only --stills t1,t2,...` (PNG, no encode), tile them with ffmpeg and inspect with vision.
4. Render: `edit_engine.py spec.json out.mp4 --preview-sheet` -> out.mp4, out_NO_MUSIC.mp4, out.beats.json, out_preview.jpg. A 20 s 1080x1920 edit takes ~40-55 s.
5. Verify: ffprobe, `ffmpeg -v error -i out.mp4 -f null -`, `-af ebur128=peak=true`.

## Dialogue / voice-over trend sounds
- librosa mis-tracks beats under speech (gave 115-129 BPM on a 140 BPM bed). Fit period+phase on the clean music part, phase-check the voice part with a comb filter over the onset envelope, then set `audio.beat_grid` {period, anchor} so cuts and drift reports use the true grid.
- Whisper word timestamps on a sound with music are off by up to ±0.5 s. For subtitle timing, transcribe growing prefixes (0..T in 0.2-0.4 s steps; a word shows up once it's complete) and/or read the subtitle change times off a reference post that uses the same audio (cross-correlate to confirm alignment).
- Black-box typed subtitles: one `texts[]` entry per phrase with `box`, `font: helvetica`, `font_index: 10`, `anim: none`, `anim_in/out: 0`. Floating hearts: `overlays[]` type `hearts` (see README additions).
- A TikTok post's own sound comes down with `yt-dlp -f audio <post url>` (128k MP3, the full in-app sound).

## Pitfalls
- opencv-python must stay <5 (5.0 removed CascadeClassifier used by focus_x:"auto").
- librosa's default onset envelope doubles the tempo on slowed/reverb songs; the engine auto-picks the most regular of full/low/mix envelopes. Check `onset_mode`/tempo in the output, and fix with `beat_mult` or `tempo_hint`.
- `ffmpeg -ss t` on the output can land on the next frame; compare frames by index (`select=eq(n,N)`).
- swscale needs `accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int` for rgb->yuv, otherwise output is ~2 levels darker (already set in the engine).
- Put text at y 0.15-0.80 (TikTok UI) and keep it off faces. Flashes sit after the vignette so they read as full white.
- Live-wallpaper sources may carry corner watermarks; keep the crop away from them.
- QA helpers in engine/: `tools_sheet.py` (timestamped N-fps contact sheet, frame-accurate via select=eq(n,N)), `shot_sheet.py` (3 frames per shot from a cut list), `tile_stills.py` (tile --stills PNGs). Don't build sheets with `-ss` + `fps=` filter: on engine MP4s it drifted 0.5-1 s and mislabelled frames.
- Black frame slot: a clip with `"grade": {"exposure": -12, "saturation": 0}` (any source). The engine has no solid-colour clip.
- 16:9 output from 16:9 sources with corner bugs (Crunchyroll top-left logo + (c) line bottom-right): static `zoom` >= 1.22 with focus_y 0.48-0.54 crops both; check punch-ins (higher zoom) with the formula top = cy - 540/zoom > logo bottom.
- Section verification: cross-correlate a hit TikTok's audio (research/*/recent_analysis/<id>/audio.wav) with the master (8 kHz mono, fftconvolve) to get the trend's exact song offset; lrclib.net synced lyrics + whisper-cli with ~/.hermes/cache/whisper/ggml-small.en.bin give usable line/word positions (not words) on rock vocals. Album-version line timings beat the producer's guess (BMTL: 'Frozen' = 2:18.0, scream 2:54.5, final chorus 2:58.6).
- explicit cuts + `snap: null` + `beat_mult: 2` (half-beats in beats.json) for quick cuts on the 'and'; write your own cuts.txt drift table (engine prints n/a without snap).
- TikTok sounds: `yt-dlp -J <post>` lists an `audio` format (mp3 128k) = the sound file itself. `yt-dlp -f audio <post>` gives clean audio with no video/dialogue mixed in. Use it before you rip post audio with ffmpeg.
- Hoodtrap/trap remixes: the tracker can lock onto the dotted hi-hats (e.g. 156 BPM instead of 116), and tempo_hint/onset_mode may not fix it. Build the grid from onset peaks (snare hits on integer beat positions), use `cuts.mode: explicit` with those times, and use explicit time lists for `beat_fx.on`. Compute the drift yourself, because the engine reports n/a without `snap`.
- Need a section longer than what follows the drop? Splice the audio yourself, beat to beat with an 8 ms crossfade (e.g. the sound's tail looped back to its start, the way TikTok loops), write a WAV and point `audio.file` at it.
- 4:3 (1440x1080) from Crunchyroll YouTube clips: the logo reaches x≈550, y≈105 and the © line sits at y>1030, x>1290. `zoom` ≥1.27 with focus_y 0.5 clears both.
- Scene-index shots can hide sub-shots and logo fades (PV montages, a title logo fading in over the last 0.8 s, a white wash on the first frames). Before you set `in`, check the exact boundaries with `trim=a:b,select='gt(scene,0.15)',showinfo` and verify every slot range against the shot bounds.
- Crossfades pre-roll the incoming clip by duration/2 before `in` and run the outgoing clip duration/2 past `out` (times speed). On short shots that bleeds the NEIGHBOURING source shot in at up to 50%. Keep `in`/`out` at least half a crossfade (+1 frame) inside the shot. Use a hard cut where there's no margin, unless the next clip is the source-adjacent shot (then the bleed is just continuous playback). Reference planner: ~/IkkimiAssets/edits/06-ff-i-found-her/v1/work/build_spec.py.
- The render's beat grid (out.beats.json) can differ 10-60 ms from an `--analyze` run on a different window. After the first render, rebuild text starts and flash times from beats.json snapped to frames (round(b*fps)/fps), so text appears on the exact cut frame.
- Soft lyric edits: Playfair Display variable serif is in engine/fonts (`font` = file path, `weight` 560). White text with a dark mauve glow halo (glow_color ~[70,34,48], radius 14) stays legible on bright pastel shots; a pink glow washes out. `anim:"zoom"` = fade in + slow 8 % grow (fade, not pop). Auto-fit the size so the line is ≤ 0.8 W.
- 1:1 output from 16:9 = a 0.5625-wide centre strip. Small artist-MV lyric text at x > 0.9 is cropped away, but faces at x < 0.25 or > 0.75 get cut (e.g. an eyes close-up loses half of each eye).
- **Difference-blend lyric words** (letters invert the footage, |footage - near-white| at ~93%; hit BC hype refs use it, and it reads designed on both dark and bright shots): engine v2 `blend` has no `difference` mode. Composite it per frame in a custom renderer: `reg = reg*(1-a) + abs(reg - tint)*a` with a = ink mask x opacity (`edits/13-bc-mashi-1to1/work/render.py` `composite_word`). On mid-grey footage the word nearly vanishes, so check those frames.
- PIL variable fonts (`Unbounded[wght]`, `Archivo[wdth,wght]`, `Saira[wdth,wght]`, `BigShouldersDisplay[wght]`): `get_variation_axes()` names the axes 'Weight'/'Width', not 'wght'/'wdth'. Map the names before `set_variation_by_axes`, and fall back to each axis's default. A wrong mapping silently renders the thinnest master, which looks like a broken font. Ref: `edits/13-bc-mashi-1to1/work/fontlab.py`. Google Fonts files download from `https://github.com/google/fonts/raw/main/ofl/<family>/<File>.ttf` (URL-encode the brackets as %5B/%5D).
- `texts[].font` takes a file path but not a .ttc index. For italic/heavy system faces (e.g. Avenir Next Heavy Italic, index 9) pull the face out with fontTools (`TTCollection(path).fonts[i].save(x.ttf)`) and keep the .ttf in the edit's assets/, because scratch gets pruned.
- For 1 fps / 4 fps critic sheets, use `select=eq(n,N)` with N = round(t*fps), not `-vf fps=1`. The fps filter samples about half an interval late, so the sheet labels end up ~0.5 s off and you'll chase phantom flash or black frames.
- Shazam the reference's sound before you trust a caption or task note about the version. ref5 (@dojii.editz 'From the Start') looked like Laufey but was the Good Kid cover (YouTube kq2BCYAKDNc). The Laufey master only reached 0.67 mel-corr; the cover gave 0.99. Hit edits also splice: chorus-1 line → the song's own stop → raw anime dialogue (find its source by fftconvolve of each chunk against all footage audio, nc>0.95) → chorus 2. Run a 0.5 s windowed correlation to find the splice points.
- When the in-app TikTok sound = the official track: confirm it with a mel-spectrogram sliding correlation of the reference post's audio against the song (try rates 0.80-1.06, because hype edits often use 0.82-0.85x slowed copies; resample the ref by the rate before correlating; a peak of ~0.9+ at one rate gives the version, e.g. 0.99 at 0.835). The best offset tells you where the TikTok sound starts.
- Lyric-word timing without whisper: sample the reference edit at 5 fps, read its word-per-beat text with vision, and map ref time to song time with the correlation offset. Then snap each word to the nearest beat and skip explicit words.
- The engine's `--stills-only` still decodes every slot, so on a loaded machine it can take ~3-4 min. A full render plus the critic pack is often just as fast.
- Crossfades are centred on the cut, so each clip plays ~duration/2 x speed before `in` and after its slot end. With sub-1 s PV shots, inset `in`/`out` by that much or the neighbouring source shot bleeds into the dissolve. Check the `[decode] src [a,b]` ranges in the log against the scene index.
- `focus_x/focus_y` is the crop centre in source fractions, clamped to the frame. To crop out a Crunchyroll watermark (1080p: x20-550, y18-102, plus a (c) line at y>=1032 on the right) in 16:9 output, use `zoom` 1.3 and `focus_y` 0.53.
- Per-clip `grade` is a shallow merge over the global grade (a clip `preset` doesn't reset the global keys). If you change the global grade, re-check every per-clip override: a fix like "sat 0.8" does nothing once the global is 0.8, and a new global `tint`/`shadows_tint` leaks into clips with their own warm preset unless you zero it there. Test grades on source PNGs with `from edit_engine import Grade; Grade(cfg)(bgr)`. It takes seconds, no render.
- Teal vs grey-blue: a negative `temperature` alone gives blue. For teal, use temperature about −0.03, `tint` about −0.07 and shadows_tint about [−0.08, 0.055, 0.045], and keep highlights slightly warm so skin and blond hair don't go olive.
- Text-on-face QA: for each text, grab its first and last frame (select=eq(n,N)) and tile them. Push-ins (ken_burns/zoom) move faces into text that was clear on the first frame.
- `freeze` effect: pass seconds as the value, `{"type":"freeze","at_seconds":0.44}`. `"at_seconds": true` is read as float(True) = 1.0 s, so the freeze silently lands at 1 s (often past the slot). Otherwise use `at` as a fraction of the slot.
- Full-body shots with the head near the top of a Crunchyroll 16:9 source: don't lower focus_y at a centred crop, because that exposes the logo (x<550, y<100). Shift the crop right past x 550 instead (zoom 1.27, focus_x ≈0.59, focus_y ≈0.395 → crop x 570-1704, y 0-850) and the subject sits at ~0.4 of the frame.
- Golden/romance grade on high-key shots (white backgrounds, sunny pastel): glow `threshold` 0.55 at strength >0.5 screen-blends the whole frame (black hair 64→101, milky look). Vignette 0.26 turns white bg grey (253→185 at the edges). Use threshold ~0.76, vignette ≤0.12 (softness 0.8), `black` 0.04, and per-clip exposure -0.2 / gamma 1.12 on very bright shots. Test variants offline: import edit_engine, apply `Grade(cfg)` + `glow()` + `make_vignette()` to source frames, and tile them (ref: edits/04-dud-golden-hour/v2/work/grade_lab*.py).
- DUD index mislabels: S07-021 ('Gojo hands on Marin's face') is Gojo holding a hina-doll head, no Marin, plus a circle wipe at 24.6. For a tender two-shot, use S10-030 (10_s2_op_reveal_pv @34.83-35.70; the 'Season 2' title card starts at 35.786). S09-092 has an internal cut at 97.555: 97.60-99.14 is the wide of the pair from behind under gold fireworks.
- Blonde Marin in neutral indoor light (S10-030) goes orange under global temperature 0.13. Give it per-clip temp ~0.05, sat 1.06.
- Footage that's already golden-hour lit (DUD Crunchyroll clip UFhXP2GUMf0): global warmth turns Marin's hair ginger. Override per clip with `temperature` 0 and sat ~1.06.
- JVKE 'this is what falling in love feels like' (Topic cGO6ZwrppDQ, 120 s): trackers/songbpm say 129, but it is 108.02 BPM (0.55547 s) with a triplet 12/8 feel (kicks 2/3 triplet-8ths apart). Chorus 2 + outro are constant tempo (drop attack 66.625); the intro and chorus 1 are free tempo. The intro pre-drop riser 15.0-15.7 is the same audio as 65.93-66.63, so you can splice intro→chorus 2 there at +50.934. Ref: edits/09-dud-dub-edit/plan/PLAN.md.
- Headless-browser TikTok tag/search pages show a slider CAPTCHA and the yt-dlp tiktok:user extractor fails, so finding the official sound can dead-end. Note it and post as original sound instead of burning time.
- JVKE 'golden hour' (master 8XZ7dKan--4): the tracker gives 191 BPM, but the real tempo is 94.3 (0.636 s). The music drops out at 45.65-46.15 and the beat phase jumps there: 'Shine' = 46.184. Pre-chorus 'I was all alone' starts at 28.72.
- Framing check: vision's face-x estimates from gridded frame strips can be off by 0.2+ of the width (v2 of edit 03 cut Gojo in half twice). Draw the real crop window with `engine/tools_cropwin.py out.jpg list.txt` (lines `label|relpath|t1,t2|zoom|focus_x|focus_y`, CROPWIN_ROOT = footage dir; the green line marks text y 0.84) and ask vision only "is a face cut by the red box / crossed by the green line".
- Monologue-over-intro, then the hook: splice the song yourself. Take intro [a0, break] + chorus [hook-ε, end], and put the join inside the intro's own silent break (-50 dB) with an 8 ms equal-power crossfade. Map an intro beat onto the chorus beat so the whole edit is on one constant `beat_grid`. If the band stops dead after the hook line (Laufey 'From the Start', 75.0-76.1 s), end there and the loop is clean.
- Soft acoustic/bossa songs: onset detectors disagree by 40-50 ms (mid-band flux peaks come earlier than broadband/RMS rise). Put the grid anchor between them (~+20 ms from the mid-band fit) so cuts stay within 1 frame of both.
- 1:1 posts: lyric text can sit at y 0.84 of the square (that's screen y ≈ 0.69 in the 9:16 player), which clears chins in close two-shots. With Playfair Italic, use letter_spacing 0; spacing > 0 splits 'feel' into 'f eel'.
- Text-off-faces QA: grab frame-accurate stills at the middle of every (text x shot) overlap. Draw translucent bands at y 0.19 and 0.79 (±0.035), then ask vision which band misses eyes/mouth. A text that spans several shots needs one band that's clear in all of them, so end it on a cut or reorder the shots. Reference: edits/01-bc-frozen-inside/v2/qa/bands.py.
- Before you use a critic-suggested in-point inside a shot you already use, look at both frames. Held anime CUs (e.g. a roar) look identical across seconds, so a 'new' in-point can be a repeated shot.
- The grade's `black` is a crush point (0 = no crush; higher = deeper blacks), not a lift. Use `lift` to raise blacks.
- Explicit cuts: `cuts: {mode: explicit, times: [beat times], snap: nearest}`. The last clip runs to `audio.end`, so set `audio.end = start + last beat` to end on a beat.
- Kinetic lyric words (what 1M+ romance edits do): one entry per word group, small italic lead words (Playfair Italic) plus a 150-210 px bold keyword (Playfair weight 800), alternating the top band (y 0.15/0.25) and bottom band (y 0.655/0.765). Use `pop_from` > 1 to slam a payoff word in, `scale_to` to grow it, and `x_to` for a slow drift. Build a face map per shot (which band is free) first. Where a cut puts a face in the band, end the word at the cut and re-enter it in the other band with `anim: none` (it reads as a kinetic jump).
- Word timing from a hit edit on a different chorus: find where the chorus repeats with a chroma self-similarity scan (chroma_cqt, cosine over a 14 s window; the repeat scores ~0.98), map ref time → chorus-1 time, take each word's appearance window (prev sampled frame, first frame], then snap to a beat/half-beat. Whisper word times on 'her' swung ±0.6 s between runs, so don't use them.
- Before you promise 'one eye centred' on an eye ECU, grab the full 16:9 frame: many ECUs clip both eyes' outer corners at the source edge. For a square crop, push zoom against the clamped edge (e.g. zoom 1.55→1.9, focus_x 0.87). The iris then drifts toward the centre as the crop shrinks.
- The engine has no source-crop key.
