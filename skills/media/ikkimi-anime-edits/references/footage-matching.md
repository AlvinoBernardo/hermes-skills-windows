# Footage matching: finding the right shot fast and correctly

Use this when filling an edit's shot slots, especially when matching shots of a reference edit.

## Fastest path to a reference's exact shot
- **Reference editors cut runs of consecutive shots from one scene.** Once one reference shot is found in the episode index, scan the neighbouring 10 s clips with a contact sheet of about 7 frames per clip before grepping descriptions. It's much faster and usually gives the exact shot.
- **Image-to-image search** (`pipeline/clip_index.py search --image ref_frame.jpg`) narrows the candidates for a reference frame. It's a shortlist only: verify the top hits by eye against the reference frame. If none fits, widen the search (top 30, neighbouring clips, text descriptions) before settling for a weaker shot.
- **Find the episode copy of an official/MV/PV shot by audio xcorr**, not by searching descriptions. Concatenate the episode's 10 s clip audio (16 kHz mono) and fftconvolve a 2 s chunk of the official clip against it. nc > 0.95 gives a constant offset. MV/PV shots usually exist in an episode without lyrics or credits.
- **Find the episode source of a preview/trailer shot by picture**: cache 48x27 grey thumbnails at 4 fps per episode and correlate one query frame; a true match scores 0.98-1.0.

## Verify before you commit
- Re-run scene detect on the chosen in/out. Index shot bounds miss soft reframes, and a slot must fit inside one continuous shot.
- Index labels have mislabels (the wrong character, the wrong shot start). Check by eye in the source, every time.
- OP sequences can contain 2-4-frame staccato zoom steps. Check frame by frame before assuming 0.3 s of continuous footage.
- A shot crossing a 10 s clip boundary is continuous: stream-copy concat the two clips (`ffmpeg -f concat -c copy`).
- Parallel pickers reuse the same plan candidates, so check the sibling pick files for duplicates before finalising.
- When grabbing check frames, `-ss t` returns the first frame at or after t. A frame at `t_end - 0.04` on 23.976 fps can already be the next shot, so sample out-frames at `out - 1.5/fps`. Scene detect (`gt(scene,0.15)`) also fires on fast head-turns inside one shot, so confirm every 'inside' hit by eye.
- Twixtor packs mix 60 fps and 30 fps files: check fps per file and set speed from it (2-2.5x on 60 fps, about 1.5x on 30 fps).
- Lip-synced dialogue inserts: confirm with a per-frame mouth-crop sheet against the audio RMS envelope. Index and whisper times can be about 0.5 s off.

## Credits and logos
- Creditless OPs still carry a song credit and a series logo in the bottom corners. Measure the boxes once per show, then crop or zoom just enough that they stay outside the frame (for Fragrant Flower: song credit x 0.02-0.40, y 0.87-0.97; logo x 0.77-0.97, y 0.89-0.98; a 1.15x zoom anchored away from them works in 16:9).
- Crunchyroll clips have a top-left bug and a bottom-right © line. A zoom of 1.27 or more clears them; grab the top edge of every shot to check.
