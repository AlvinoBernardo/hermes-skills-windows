# The user's anime-edit song bank

The user knows the anime-edit niche from the inside. These songs are known to go viral on **romance / non-shonen** edits.

**How to use it:** it's a fallback and a sanity check, not a mandatory list. Pick songs by your own judgment first and try new songs freely, because many viral romance edits use songs outside this list. Reach for the bank when you're unsure or have no good pick. If a pick is bad, the user will say so; then swap in one from here. Obscure scraped "rising" trend sounds were the picks he rejected, so lean on known songs or the show's own music when in doubt.

**Version check:** confirm the exact recording before downloading, because edits often use a cover or a sped-up version (e.g. "From the Start" edits used the Good Kid cover, not Laufey's master). Entries marked (verify) are best guesses at the title and artist.
