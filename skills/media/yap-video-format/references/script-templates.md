# Script templates: 7 proven yap structures

Each template has its beats, the slot types, the original hit, and the user-shaped output (word-for-word hook, then beat bullets he can say in his own words). The outlier is views divided by the median of the surrounding posts.

---

## 1. "I reverse engineered / I tried X — here are the top 3 things I found" (bold claim reveal)
**Source:** "I reverse engineered Hinge" (417k, 37x, 89 s).
1. **Hook:** "I [did a bold technical thing] to [famous app/brand] and here are the top three things I found."
2. **Context in one breath:** the trendy tool or method that made it possible ("there's a GitHub repo blowing up called Reverse Engineer Anything").
3. **Disclaimer joke** to build trust: "we can only see the user-facing binary. If we want their algorithm we either get a job there or do something illegal."
4. **Thing one / two / three:** each is one surprising, concrete fact plus a "you" scenario ("Have you ever screenshotted a hot girl's profile and sent it to the boys? Hinge knows.").
5. **Anticlimax:** "I thought I'd find some crazy juice, but nope, it's a regular app."
6. **Bridge plus sign-off.**

**Warning:** his Tinder remake with the same format got 0.54x. The brand and the first fact carry this template, so pick the brand people are already curious or paranoid about.

## 2. "Here's my whole system in 60 seconds" (system walkthrough / AI stack)
**Sources:** "Every video on my page is edited by Claude Code" (300k, 26.85x), "Here's the AI stack I use as a solo founder" (58k, 5.7x), "Overnight agents 101" (124k, 12x), "AI Agents 101" (125k, 10.4x).
1. **Hook:** either a meta reveal ("This video you're watching… is edited by AI") or loss aversion ("If you pay for Claude/ChatGPT you're wasting your money unless…").
2. **Promise:** "Here is my whole system in 60 seconds" / "by the end of this video you'll know how to…".
3. **Credibility receipt:** "To establish credibility, here's my AI usage in the last 30 days" (screenshot of tokens and $).
4. **Numbered steps (5-7),** each with one tool named plus one concrete number ("a document of 255 proven viral hooks", "84 tests, 38 bugs in one night").
5. **Analogy for the abstract idea:** a store manager with $1,500 of authority, or AI idle overnight being "like a gym membership you never use".
6. **Absurd CTA:** "Comment real and nothing will happen because I'm too cheap for ManyChat. Paste this video into your Claude and tell it to make no mistakes."

**Best fit for this user:** vibe-coding / AI-agent / content-system videos. Use his REAL numbers only.

## 3. "Everyone knows X, but nobody talks about Y" (famous-person breakdown)
**Sources:** "Everyone knows Roy Lee… a lot less is known about his co-founder Neel" (989k, 167x), and the Prosper Chiu Hinge-experiment reaction (111k, 10.4x).
1. **Hook:** "Everyone knows [famous person / company], but a lot less is known about [overlooked person/angle]. And this dude is the entire reason [opinion]."
2. **Baseline:** the famous person's impressive stats, rattled off fast.
3. **Escalating stat stack** on the overlooked one, each stat followed by a deflating joke ("He qualified for AIME five times. Yes, five is a larger number than two.").
4. **Insider aside** that gives you authority ("I went to this school too. It's where dreams go to die.").
5. **Kicker:** "and this is only what I found on his LinkedIn".
6. **Mog punchline:** "In summary, Neel mogs Roy in both intelligence and height."

**Receipts:** a whiteboard listing the stats, photos and profile cards. Tag both people in the caption, because the subjects may repost. Ride a person who is trending this week.

## 4. Stat → twist (controversial stat / chart fear)
**Sources:** "Asian women are the most sought after by men of every race except…" (461k, 117x, 20 s), "This chart right here is why you're going to be homeless, jobless, and die alone" (91k, 8.5x), "why asian women > everything else" (163k, 24x, 94 s).
1. **Hook:** the shocking stat or the chart, said as fact in the first 3 words.
2. **Source:** the book, study or app it came from, one line.
3. **Your reaction or personal angle:** "I'm half-Asian half-white. Thanks, statistics. Very cool."
4. **Relatable pain beat** (for the chart version): "we apply to 400 places, get two OAs, one gets to the 13th round, then ghosted".
5. **Twist ending:** a bait-and-switch ("now that I got all the white dudes here, who should I listen to to get into jazz fusion?") or a reveal ("Oh wait, this is the dating chart. Oops.").

**Short (20-40 s) works here.** The twist makes people rewatch and comment. For this user, find stats he can own (CS jobs, AI usage, app-store, fitness/calorie myths, founder revenue) and keep the twist harmless.

## 5. "I gave up / I burned [huge number]… and [unexpected result]" (personal stakes story)
**Sources:** "I gave up a pretty decent shot at a $3M/yr salary" (224k, 34x), "I burned 6 billion AI tokens this weekend and it got me a job interview at a $13B startup" (248k, 23x).
1. **Hook:** a sacrifice or spend with a giant number, plus the surprising consequence.
2. **Rewind:** "Rewinding two days…" / "So a few months ago I'm sitting in the library having a full-on existential crisis."
3. **Underdog framing:** "250 people, 90% working professionals… all of us are UT freshmen".
4. **Grind details with specific numbers:** 108 LeetCode problems, a 7-hour sleep that turned into an all-nighter, 329 PRs, 9 TestFlight builds.
5. **Turning point or win,** then a joke ("we're low-key the sole reason AI uses as much water as it does").
6. **One-line advice:** "the idea is 99% of the game".
7. **Cope or deflating sign-off:** "Your first business is never about making money. It's about getting good. At least that's how I'm coping." / "Hashtag agentic or something. Bye."

## 6. Rage-bait title → defensible argument (hot take)
**Sources:** "Why every company should be abusing child labor" ("My company only hires minors, and here's why", 27k, 3.05x; the soft version got 1.53x), and "You can go from quant to entrepreneurship, but you can't go back" (37k, 5.1x).
1. **Hook:** the provocative claim stated as your policy or truth, plus "and here's why".
2. **Bit:** a caricature of the group ("your average Bay Area sixth grader has a 1580 SAT… the one thing they're missing is an unpaid internship").
3. **Your experience as evidence:** "I applied to 78 internships with tailored resumes. Zero replies."
4. **Pre-empt the objection:** "Before you say it's unethical: welcome to the game, my friend."
5. **The actual logic with a number:** "A 16-year-old and a 28-year-old both have two years of Claude Code experience. Why not hire the cheaper one?"
6. **Self-aware close:** "So that's my wisdom that I impart onto my audience of 5,000 followers."

## 7. Philosophy formula explained with absurd examples (quote/explainer)
**Source:** "Anxiety is simply intelligence minus courage" (18.5k, 2.55x; title "how to stop overthinking").
1. **Hook:** a short formula or quote stated flatly, then "So what does that mean?"
2. **Edge cases of the formula** with funny real examples (a zero-intelligence example: a famous gambler, "one of my personal heroes").
3. **Your proof:** "I have way more courage to post today than 3 months ago because I've done it 70 times."
4. **Self-roast line:** "I am a scared little b-word in the face of literally every new opportunity."
5. **Moral:** "Just start."

**Warning:** borrowed business frameworks with no personal angle flopped. Hormozi's value equation got 0.41x and NPS 0.58x. The formula must be about the viewer's feelings or life, not business theory.

---

## Low-reach formats (use knowingly)
- **Day-N diary update** ("Alright, day 36, classes, work…"): 0.81x median, 0 hits out of 35. Use it only to keep a streak alive, and open it with a self-aware punchline ("I love LARPing at job fairs." = 2.7x), not the day number.
- **Product FAQ / feature update / recruiting / contest:** 0.6-0.9x.
