# Proven hook formulas, ranked (talking videos only)

These formulas are distilled from the 229 proven hooks in `hook-bank.md`: 116 Instagram reels from @minolee.mp4 and 113 TikToks from @mino.mp4. All of them are talking videos (talking head, voiceover b-roll, talking vlog). No skits, memes, music montages or the @mino1ee comedy account.

**Stats format:** in `63x · 5.8M · IG`, 63x means 63 times his usual views (the median of the ~30 posts around it), 5.8M is the raw view count, and IG/TT is Instagram or TikTok.

**Platform split:**
- **Instagram** rewards challenges, life stories and broad relatable takes. `challenge_experiment` has a 2.16x median and 52% of them hit 2x.
- **TikTok** rewards creator tactics with proof. `result_proof_howto` has a 1.98x median and 48% hit 2x.
- On Instagram a curiosity gap (1.77x vs 0.86x without) and visible stakes (1.37x vs 0.90x) are what work. A number by itself does nothing there (0.86x vs 1.01x), though it does on TikTok (1.77x vs 0.84x).

**Placeholders:** `[ ]` marks a slot. Fill proof slots ONLY with the user's real numbers.

**Examples:** **F** = founder / personal brand (vibe coding, building an app, creator life). **N** = Noxy (calorie tracking, weight loss, fitness). Never call Noxy free.

## Tier S: the account-defining hits

### 1. The "is it possible" challenge, filmed live (Instagram's #1)
`Is it possible to [hard outcome] in [tiny time]? [Stakes: deadline / clock / $0 counter]. Let's find out.`
- **Originals:**
  - "Is it possible to ace a final after studying for just one hour? My final is tomorrow…" **63x · 5.8M · IG**. He reran it 4 times: 60x, 39x, 27x, 4x.
  - "…get one of the big prizes at an arcade" **52x · 4.6M · IG**
  - "…a really good chest, tricep and shoulder workout in just 3 minutes" **28x · 3.4M · IG**
  - "…start a business in one day and make $1,000" with a $0 counter + timestamp on screen **24x · 3.2M · IG**
  - "…learn a backflip in 1 hour", first frame mid-air **20x · 2.7M · IG**
  - "22 days ago I started a brand new account to see if I could make $10,000 in 30 days" **12x · 2.5M · IG**
- **Rules:**
  - The challenge happens NOW, with a clock or counter on screen.
  - Finish the challenge in the first ~15 s of the video.
  - The stakes are visible in frame 1.
- **What hurt it:**
  - Looking back at a past experiment: 0.73x.
  - A vague goal: 0.99x.
  - Repeats: the money challenge decayed 2.9x → 1.0x → 0.97x.
  - On TikTok the type only got 0.76x.
- F: "Is it possible to build and ship an app in one day? It's 9am and I have zero lines of code."
- N: "Is it possible to lose 2 lbs in one week without being hungry? Day 1, weigh-in on camera."

### 2. The definitive explainer, with a visual twist
`Here's the only video you'll ever need to watch to understand [system]. No, [common myth] isn't it.`
- **Originals:**
  - "Here's the only video you will ever need to watch to understand TikTok's algorithm…" **347x · 3.8M · TT**. By 2 s he cuts to himself in a suit playing "Mr. Algorithm".
  - "Here is the one thing you need to know if you actually want to blow up on Instagram, and no, it's not your niche" on a rooftop ledge **13x · 1.3M · IG**
- The costume or setting did the work. A bare repost in 2026 got 0.29x.
- F: "Here's the only video you'll ever need to understand how apps make money. No, it's not ads."
- N: "Here's the only video you'll ever need to understand why you're not losing weight. No, it's not your metabolism."

### 3. Good thing, bad feeling (villain-edge confession)
`I can't afford [basic need], so instead of [healthy move], I started [toxic reaction].` · `I regret [universally good thing], or more specifically, the way I [did it].` · `I'm processing the realization that I've been [doing X wrong] for the past [time].`
- **Originals:**
  - "I can't afford to pay rent for me and my girlfriend, so instead of asking her to split the rent, I started to just resent her for it" **169x · 3.2M · TT** and **13x · 984k · IG**
  - "I'm processing the realization that I've been taking my life too seriously", sitting on an airport floor **142x · 1.5M · TT**
  - "I regret improving myself" **117x · TT**
  - "For the past month I've been in one of the biggest slumps of my life", lying in bed **12.5x · 1.5M · IG**
  - "Four years ago I thought the meaning of life was getting a girlfriend" **10x · 1.8M · IG**
- **Rule:** open in the present with a surprising, slightly unflattering admission.
- **What flops:**
  - Past-tense backstory openers: "Two years ago I had less than $10,000…" 0.35x, "When I was 17…" 0.49x.
  - Plain "money made me sad": 0.41x.
  - TikTok story_confession overall has a 0.64x median and 42% flop.
- F: "I quit my job to build an app, and instead of being grateful I started resenting every friend with a salary."
- N: "I regret losing 20 pounds, or more specifically, the way I did it."

### 4. Pain callout, then flip or fix
`If you want to [goal] but you're afraid [specific fear], don't worry, [the fear will come true].` · `If you've been [exact thing the viewer is doing right now] and you feel like [exact feeling]… give me 60 seconds.` · `Here's how to get out of a slump.`
- **Originals:**
  - "If you want to start creating content but you're afraid people will judge you… don't worry, they will." **96x · 2.6M · TT**
  - "Here's how to get out of a slump…" over b-roll of him scrolling in bed **55x · 1.5M · IG**, and again at 10.7x
  - "If you've been laying in bed scrolling for the past hour and you feel like you've just ruined the rest of your day…" **16x · 1.5M · IG**
- **Rules:**
  - Call out what they're doing RIGHT NOW, plus a short time ask.
  - Broad identity callouts only got 0.95x.
  - The fear with no relief ("your fear of being perceived will never go away") got 0.25x.
  - **Warning:** the fear-flip line itself is a ONE-HIT WONDER. He used it 5 times and only the first went viral (then 1.42x, 0.71x, 0.44x). Use the structure, but never present it as a proven reusable hook (see reused-hooks.md).
- F: "If you want to launch your app but you're scared nobody will download it, don't worry, nobody will. At first."
- N: "If you just ate a whole bag of chips and feel like you ruined your diet… give me 30 seconds."

### 5. The guilty pleasure is actually good / the virtue is overrated
`Here's why [thing people feel guilty about] is actually GOOD for you.` · `I don't know who needs to hear this, but [quitting the guilty thing / working harder] is not going to [goal].`
- **Originals:**
  - "Here's why No Nut November is actually GOOD for you" **74x · 1.7M · IG**
  - "…the idea that you can change your life by simply working harder is bullsh*t" **21x · 2.1M · IG**
  - "…uninstalling video games is not going to make you successful" **16x · 2.6M · IG**
- Defend the guilty pleasure; don't attack it. "Better to run a scam business than…" only got 1.1x.
- N: "Here's why eating carbs at night is actually GOOD for your weight loss."

### 6. POV: fix-my-life sprint / life moment with a payoff
`POV: watch me unf*ck my life in 1 morning. It is currently [1:36 AM].` · `POV: u and bro realize u need to lock in at 2am` · `My parents don't know yet, but…`
- **Originals:**
  - "POV: Watch me unf*ck my life up in 1 morning…", watch held to the camera **29x · 1.8M · IG**
  - "My parents don't know yet, but we actually have a 5-day break…" **28x · 1.8M · IG**
  - "POV: surprising my girlfriend with first class tickets from Korea" **24x · 3.5M · IG**
  - "6:05AM, this blanket is so warm… or maybe we just quit all this entrepreneurship stuff" **17x · IG**
- A plain "day in the life" with no payoff or secret sits around 0.8x.

### 7. The ladder: every level of [pain]
`How to escape every level of [pain state]` with on-screen chapters (`[tier 1] - 0:08 · [tier 2] - 0:25 · …`)
- **Originals:**
  - "This is how to break out of every level of view jail…" with a timestamped 100 / 1K / 10K / 100K list on screen **139x · 3.8M · TT** and **10x · 645k · IG**
- The same pain as a no-text greenscreen parody got 0.16x. The remake "4 levels of view jail" got 0.68x.
- F: "How to escape every level of 'nobody uses my app': 0 users, 10, 100, 1,000."
- N: "How to break every weight-loss plateau: the first 5 lb, the first 10, the last 5."

### 8. My specific tactic + "every single time" / "Here's how to actually…"
`Here's how I [one narrow thing] so [result] every single time.` · `Here's how to actually [big outcome]. Focus on [counterintuitive principle].`
- **Originals:**
  - captions "…so they go viral every single time" **93x · 1.8M · TT**
  - "Here's how to actually blow up on TikTok. Focus on quality, not quantity." **164x · 1.8M · TT**
  - "Here's how to actually focus and get your work done 5 times as quickly" **9.7x · 614k · IG**
- A narrow tactic beats a broad promise. "How to come up with viral video ideas" got 0.35x.
- F: "Here's how I prompt Claude so my app ships with zero bugs every single time."
- N: "Here's how I log my meals in under 10 seconds so I never skip a day."

## Tier A: repeatable winners

9. **Quotable rule + huge proof.** `This [mindset hack] got me [real result]: "[short rule]."` Original: "just post and forget it, bro" **85x · TT**. Don't give away a cliché as the rule in line 1: the IG remake got 0.4x. F: "This one rule got my app to [N] users: 'Ship it ugly.'"
10. **SPEEDRUN / goal + proof on screen.** `How to SPEEDRUN [milestone]. I've done it [N] times, the fastest in [time].` Originals:
    - **84x · 1.6M · TT**
    - "About a year and a half ago I set two very ambitious goals…" with the dated goals note on screen **17x · 1.2M · IG**
    - "I started a business in college 52 days ago with a goal to make $20,000…" **13x · IG**

    Without proof, and with a smaller goal, it got 0.23x.
11. **The win flipped.** `It feels like once I stopped [chasing X], that's when [X] actually happened.` Originals: **19x · 1.2M · IG**. "I stopped doing rigid self-improvement and made way more progress" **8.9x · IG**.
12. **The weird hack, tested on myself.** `Apparently if you [weird hack], it makes you [extreme result]. So I tried it.` Originals: caffeine nap **26x · 3.6M · IG**. "Drink water immediately after waking up" **16x · IG**. The creatine version on the TikTok business account got 0.1x. It only works where the audience wants life content.

Other originals: "8 signs of a low IQ content creator" **28x · TT** and "grown man posting to 900 views" **13x · TT**. Insulting the viewer directly flops: "your competition is imbeciles" got 0.14x.

## Applies to every formula
- Frame 1 must show the stakes or proof: a clock or counter, the before state, the famous face, a screenshot, a costume. Static close-ups with small captions are most of the bottom 5 in every batch.
- 30-60 s had the best hit rate on both platforms (IG 27%, TT 35%). Talking videos over 100 s mostly sat below 0.8x, except challenges.
- Formulas decay when he repeats them back-to-back. Re-skin the topic and the visual rather than re-running the same hook.
