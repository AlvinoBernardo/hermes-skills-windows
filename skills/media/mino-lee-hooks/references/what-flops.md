# What flops: check every draft against this

## Patterns that reliably flop (each one shows up again and again across 1,139 talking videos: 600 IG reels + 539 TikToks)
1. **"Day N of..." series hooks.** series_episode: 0.77x median on IG with a 9% hit rate (65 reels), and 0.74x on TikTok with 3% (35 videos). The exceptions changed the format, e.g. an 8 s stat card ("Crashouts: 0.5", 6.6x), or led with a quotable line before the day label ("I don't think pressure creates diamonds. It creates quitters.", 3.2x). Day 1 of a challenge with real stakes ("$30k in 3 days or my business goes bankrupt") can work; later episodes never do. Lead with the episode's single best moment instead.
2. **Re-posting or re-running a hook to the same audience.** On IG: the "$402k" money reveal went 3.4x → 0.7 → 0.57 → 0.39x; dropout stories 4.0x (2024) → 0.48x (2026); 3 Claude-editing posts in 2 days went 4.2 → 3.3 → 0.74x; the "view jail" remake got 0.68x. The 3.8M "only video you'll ever need to understand TikTok's algorithm" got 2.8k (0.29x) when re-posted in 2026, and the 3.8M "every level of view jail" got 9k when re-posted 3 months later. Re-skin the formula with a new topic or visual; don't re-upload it.
3. **Mild or journal-style confessions.** "Sometimes it's not easy to show up on camera" 0.32x, "Whatever you chase really does run away from you" 0.27x, "Ever since my business hit $50k/month I stopped being present" 0.33x. Winning confessions have a twist and a slightly unflattering admission (formula #3).
4. **Question openers with nothing at stake.** "Should productivity be worshiped as a virtue?" 0.23x, "What if I try content and I FAIL?" 0.11x. question_hook: 6% hit rate. Only myth-tests win ("Do hashtags actually...?").
5. **Bleak framing with no relief.** "Your fear of being perceived will never go away" 0.25x. Losses paired with a sermon: "lost $40,000 on crypto... so grateful" 0.18x. Flip it (formula #4).
6. **Insulting the viewer or punching down.** "your competition is imbeciles" 0.14x, slurs and slang ("retardmaxxing") 0.13x, polarizing manosphere topics 0.06x. Playful identity roasts are fine ("8 signs of a low IQ content creator" 28x).
7. **Flexes with no payoff for the viewer.** "$100,000 a month is not enough" 0.43x, "I spent $43,400 today" 0.45x. Money works when it is proof for a lesson or comes with an unexpected emotion.
8. **Off-niche posts on a niche account.** Dance series (0.06-0.15x), creatine (0.10x), room tour (0.20x), crypto (0.15x), girlfriend story told as plain relationship content (0.33x). Stay on the promise the account built. On mino.mp4, self_improvement had a 0.76x median and content_creation 1.19x.
9. **Slow or backstory openers.** "So in the beginning of 2024..." 0.68x; "Aight, these are the three levels..." 0.31x; starting mid-sentence 0.33x. The first sentence IS the hook.
10. **Vague or generic advice everyone has heard.** "3 mindset tips" 0.41x, "only 2 things: value and story" 0.34x, "what it takes to genuinely succeed" 0.82x, "how to get 10k followers FAST" 0.23x.
11. **Weak first frame.** Dark or night shots, shaky glasses POV, eyes down at a laptop, faceless b-roll, tiny captions, no text hook (7% hit rate vs 27% with one). Winners show a big centered text hook plus proof or a prop in frame 1: an analytics screenshot, a dashboard, a costume, a famous face.
12. **Jargon or abstract topics.** "Your personal brand and cognitive dissonance" 0.26x; theta waves 0.32x; a Nassim Taleb quote 0.31x.
13. **Too many stacked identity details.** "Let me guess, you're the League of Legends Asian who got burnt out... SAT hagwon..." 0.37x. Pick one identity.
14. **Answer given away in sentence 1.** "it's learning to talk to a camera" 0.55x, "treat business like a video game" 0.53x. Keep the loop open ("…and we just made…", 12x).
15. **Length.** 30-60 s has the best hit rate (IG 27%, TT 35%). TikTok talking videos under 30 s hit 2x only 13% of the time. Talking videos over 100 s mostly sit under 0.8x unless they are a challenge.
16. **Lookback challenges and vague goals.** "Is it possible" works live with a clock. Retelling a past experiment got 0.73x; "launch a tech startup… make money day one" got 0.99x; a low-stakes handstand got 0.81x.
17. **Paid ads skew the numbers.** An ad on his account (a Shopify reel, 2.4M, 31x) is excluded from the stats; ignore ad-boosted posts when copying.
